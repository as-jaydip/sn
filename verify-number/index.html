Verify a Number

Learn how to check a call or message claiming to be from StockNexa before you respond.

StockNexa has not provided an official phone number for publication on this page. This page cannot look up or verify a number.

How to check an unexpected contact

  1. Do not call back using a number, link, or payment instruction included in an unexpected call or message.
  2. Visit stocknexa.in by entering the address yourself, or use the contact details on the Contact StockNexa page.
  3. Ask StockNexa through an independently obtained contact channel whether the call or message is genuine.
  4. Do not rely on caller ID, a messaging profile name, or a search result alone to prove who contacted you.

Warning signs

  • Pressure to act immediately, keep the conversation secret, or make an unusual payment.
  • Requests for a password, PIN, CVV, one-time passcode, or remote access to your device.
  • Promises of guaranteed investment returns or claims that you must pay to unlock a guaranteed profit.
  • Payment instructions that do not match details independently confirmed through StockNexa's official website.

Report a suspicious call or message

You can contact the support address listed on our Contact StockNexa page: support@stocknexa.in. Include the caller's number or account name, the date and time, and a brief description. Do not send passwords, one-time passcodes, complete card or bank details, or identity documents.

If you shared sensitive information

Contact your bank or payment provider using the number on its official website or payment card. Change any exposed passwords through the service's official website, and report the incident to StockNexa using the independently obtained support details above.

This is safety guidance only. It does not validate phone numbers or query an official verification service.
