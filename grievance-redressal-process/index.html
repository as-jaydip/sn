Grievance Redressal Process

We aim to provide a clear, respectful process for receiving and reviewing concerns.

How to raise a grievance

  1. Contact StockNexa through the verified support channel and describe the issue.
  2. Include a service or complaint reference, if available, and your preferred contact method.
  3. Keep the acknowledgement or case reference so that you can follow up.
  4. If the concern is unresolved, use the next escalation level in the escalation matrix.

Response and escalation

Response times, officer details and escalation channels must be published only after confirmation by StockNexa.
